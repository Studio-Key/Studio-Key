Copy-only structure for a landing page. Not part of the Studio Key hosting site.

## Brief for whoever builds this

- **Who it's for:** owners and managers of waste operators, arriving from Richard Devenport's introductory email.
- **What the page does:** gives context, handles objections and lets them watch the full video. The system is a bespoke digital system that lets drivers manage collections, waste transfer notes and weighbridge tickets from their phones.
- **End goal:** they call Richard or fill in the short form.
- **Voice:** matter of fact. State things as they are. British English. Speak to the business owner as "your drivers", "your office", "your customers", never "the driver". Richard is named in buttons, the footer and the support answer only. No em dashes, no sales slogans, no invented figures.

  - No eyebrow text, anywhere.
  - One testimonial only.
  - The heading in section 1 is the page's single `h1`, for accessibility and SEO.
- **Context from the email:** Richard Devenport is from Weldon, Northamptonshire. The system was introduced in a 2-minute video. Documentation can be sent automatically to customers once approved. The government's Digital Waste Tracking requirements start in October 2027.

## 2. What the system does

**H2:** Each collection is recorded once, then kept together

1. Your office books the collection, or your customer requests it online.
2. Your driver opens the job on their phone and records the waste, the weights and the customer's signature.
3. The completed paperwork reaches your office straight away for approval.
4. Once you approve it, your customer receives their documents automatically.

**H2:** What your business gets

| Today | With the system |

| Weighbridge tickets stay in cabs and never reach the office | Weighbridge tickets are recorded on the phone and reach your office straight away |
